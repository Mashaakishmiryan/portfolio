About Me

More than one direction

Hello, I'm Maral Kishmiryan. I was born in 2004 in Armenia and graduated from the National Polytechnic University of Armenia, where I studied Software Engineering.

My professional journey has developed across technology, design, and quality assurance. I have experience working as a Manual QA specialist and as a UI/UX Designer, where I learned to combine logical thinking with creativity.

Today, I work in a car import company, creating banners and visual content for digital platforms. This experience has allowed me to develop my visual design skills while working in a fast-paced business environment.

I enjoy working with digital products, creating visual experiences, discovering how things work, and continuously learning new skills.

"For me, technology and creativity are different parts of the same process: understanding a problem, finding an idea, creating something, testing it, and making it better."
